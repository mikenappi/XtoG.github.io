Imagine this copy of a book had never been printed. Its pages could have contained exactly the same sentences if printed tomorrow. An account of what the book would be still leaves a question about why a copy exists. The argument begins with that gap.

## The route through the argument

1. [Contingency and dependence](../argument/contingency/) asks what licenses the move from *could have been absent* to *depends on a ground*.
2. [A necessary foundation](../argument/necessity/) asks whether a complete explanation can consist entirely of dependent things.
3. [A mental foundation](../argument/mind/) asks how the same necessary ground could account for different possible outcomes.
4. [The final conclusion](../argument/conclusion/) states how much follows if those steps succeed.

## Reading this edition

The chapters reconstruct the argument in prose. Premise numbers in the [objections](../examination/objections/) refer to the earlier outline. The [formal reconstruction](../examination/proof/) gives each inference a form you can challenge independently.
