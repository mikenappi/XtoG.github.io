title: The final conclusion
---

**P22.** At least one independent ground of dependent reality is itself the ultimate ground that settles which order obtains.

P22 identifies a single ground with both explanatory roles. The existence of two distinct contributors would not establish that identification.

**C7.** Contingent reality has a necessary mental foundation.

By C4, C6, and P22, one and the same ground exists necessarily and has mental capacity.
