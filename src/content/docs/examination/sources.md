title: Sources and further reading
---

These readings supply background for evaluating the argument. The numbered outline and the replies on this site are a reconstruction of the author's working argument.

- **Principle of Sufficient Reason.** The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy surveys versions of the principle and disagreements about its scope. Read alongside P5 when deciding which facts call for explanation. [Read the entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sufficient-reason/).
- **Cosmological Argument.** The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy discusses contingency arguments, objections to explaining the universe as a whole, and the further inference to a personal cause. Read alongside Parts II and III. [Read the entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/cosmological-argument/).
