---
title: Glossary
---

**Contingent existence.** Something actually exists and could have failed to exist. The alternative must be metaphysically possible. Our uncertainty about its existence would establish a weaker claim.

**Necessary existence.** Something exists in every metaphysically possible circumstance.

**Ground.** That in virtue of which something exists or a fact obtains. A ground can be explanatory even when it and what it grounds exist at the same time.

**Intrinsic nature.** What a thing is in itself. The argument asks whether this nature could fully account for the thing's existence.

**Restricted Principle of Sufficient Reason (PSR).** Every instance of contingent existence has a sufficient explanation for its actuality.

**Dependent totality.** All dependent existents considered together. Speaking collectively leaves open whether there is a further object composed of all of them.

**Contrastive explanation.** An account of why this outcome occurred when another was possible.

**Intentional selection.** An agent settles which possibility becomes actual in light of an end or reason. It requires the capacity to represent alternatives.
