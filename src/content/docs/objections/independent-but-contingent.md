---
title: "An independent existent cannot be contingent, since C2 would give it an external ground."
---

**Challenge to P12.** “Nothing else grounds it” and “it could not have failed to exist” express different claims. Why could the independent [ground](../../terms/ground--objections-independent-but-contingent-1/) not be a brute contingent existent?

**Reply.** Assume it were both independent and contingent. P2 excludes a wholly intrinsic sufficient explanation. P5 requires a sufficient explanation. Its ground must then be external, contrary to the assumption. This is a conditional proof whose force tracks the defenses of P2 and P5. Defining independence more emphatically would add nothing.
