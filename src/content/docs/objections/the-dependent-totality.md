---
title: The dependent totality
---

**Challenge to P8–P10.** Explaining every member may exhaust what needs explanation. Asking for another ground can sound like counting the collection twice.

**Reply.** Grant every member its local explanation. Those explanations tell us how one member can exist given others. The collective question asks why the condition they all draw on obtains at all. No extra object need be postulated to ask it. The disagreement concerns whether the collective question adds a genuine explanatory demand. An opponent who denies that demand can accept every local relation without accepting the conclusion.
